# Changelog

## 1.1.0 - 2026-10-28
- New option "Show the Nearby tab" (Options > Mods > Container tabs): untick it to hide the Nearby tab.
- The "better than what you wear" arrow now only appears on items that deserve a top indicator, and clothing is compared by protection alone, so warmth no longer hides it from a more protective piece.
- Fixed the arrow missing on protective clothing (boots, for example) until it was moved to another container.
- Fixed the selected tab being left out of view when the window is made narrower.
- Fixed all the tabs disappearing at certain window widths (this also needs Paravel's UI 1.4.1).
