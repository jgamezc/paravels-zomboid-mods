title=Paravel's Enhanced Inventory
description=A grid view for the inventory and loot windows, with colour-coded item families, a tab for everything nearby and themes.
description=
description=[h2]What it does[/h2]
description=Replaces the inventory and loot windows' list with a grid of icons, so you see more at once and find things by shape and colour. The game keeps doing the actual work: menus, transfers, drag and drop and double click are the game's own.
description=
description=[h2]Features[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*] [b]Grid, list or compact.[/b] One button in the title bar cycles the views (compact is the grid in a bare window). Each window remembers its view, its sort order and its column width.
description=[*] [b]Colour by what an item is for.[/b] Every item is tinted by its family (combat, food, medical, clothing, workshop, survival, knowledge, materials, electric, mechanics), so a bag of mixed loot reads at a glance. A bar under the icon shows its remaining condition.
description=[*] [b]Top indicator.[/b] A small band on the cell that says what stands out in a family: a weapon among ammo, or food you can eat right now (not rotten, nothing that needs cooking, and no strong unhappiness, thirst or stress).
description=[*] [b]Upgrade arrow.[/b] A green arrow on clothes and bags that protect, warm or carry more than what you wear in that slot, and on weapons that hit harder than what you hold or have attached.
description=[*] [b]Skill book volume.[/b] A Roman numeral (I to V) on skill books, so you know which volume it is without opening the tooltip.
description=[*] [b]Nearby tab.[/b] In the loot window, everything in the containers around you in one place, instead of clicking through each one.
description=[*] [b]Equipped tab.[/b] In your inventory, only what you wear or hold.
description=[*] [b]Transfer all and take all[/b] buttons next to the sort button, and sorting by group, name, category or weight in every view.
description=[*] [b]Easier container swaps.[/b] While you drag an item over the tabs, the open tab hides its name so more tabs fit and you can reach the one you want.
description=[*] [b]Collapsed window.[/b] Just the container's name and a bar with how full it is.
description=[*] [b]Options and themes.[/b] In the game's Mods options: icon size and padding, stack count style, and a switch for the top indicator, the condition bar, the background colour, the upgrade arrow and the volume numeral. Pick a theme there (Paravel's UI) and the whole window follows it.
description=[/list]
