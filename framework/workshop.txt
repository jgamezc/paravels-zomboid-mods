description=[h2]What it is[/h2]
description=The base of [b]Paravel's Enhanced Inventory[/b], [b]Paravel's Enhanced Context Menu[/b] and [b]Paravel's Enhanced Tooltips[/b]. It holds the theme they share and the drawing and controls they are built from (icon buttons, scroll bar, progress bar, window frame). You only need it alongside one of those mods.
description=
description=[h2]Themes[/h2]
description=Pick a theme once in the game's Mods options (Paravel's UI) and every window those mods restyle follows it. There are ten, light and dark:
description=[list]
description=[*] [b]Classic[/b]: close to the game's own windows.
description=[*] [b]Glass[/b]: frosted, see-through slate with round corners.
description=[*] [b]Legible[/b]: opaque white on black, made to be read.
description=[*] [b]Military[/b]: olive drab and khaki.
description=[*] [b]Neon[/b]: near-black violet with cyan and magenta.
description=[*] [b]Paper[/b]: a field notebook, cream with brown ink.
description=[*] [b]Ruin[/b]: warm charcoal and bone white.
description=[*] [b]Snow[/b]: frosted blue-white with slate text.
description=[*] [b]Terminal[/b]: an old phosphor screen in greens.
description=[*] [b]Vivid[/b]: pure black and white with full-strength colours, for accessibility.
description=[/list]
