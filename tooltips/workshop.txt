description=[h2]What it does[/h2]
description=Restyles the game's tooltips in the theme you pick: the window frame, the item's name and the rows of what it says, with bars where the game shows a bar. Food, drinks and fluids, clothing, weapons, books and media, containers and gadgets are rebuilt in Lua to do it.
description=
description=An item this mod cannot be sure about, such as one from another mod, keeps the game's own tooltip, inside the theme's frame.
description=
description=[h2]Themes[/h2]
description=Pick a theme once in the game's Mods options (Paravel's UI); every tooltip follows it, together with the other Paravel's mods.
description=
