description=[h2]What it does[/h2]
description=Restyles the right-click menus, submenus included, in the theme you pick:
description=[list]
description=[*] A window frame around the menu, and the hovered option as a rounded fill.
description=[*] Text in the theme's colours: muted when an option is disabled, green or red where the game marks an option as good or bad.
description=[*] Clean icons in place of the "<" and ">" marks, the tick and the scroll arrows.
description=[/list]
description=The options, their icons and what they do are the game's own.
description=
description=[h2]Themes[/h2]
description=Pick a theme once in the game's Mods options (Paravel's UI); every menu follows it, together with the other Paravel's mods.
description=
description=[h2]Requirements[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*] Project Zomboid Build 42.21 or later.
description=[*] [b]Paravel's UI[/b], the framework this mod is built on.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*] Part of a family of mods that share Paravel's UI and its themes, together with [b]Paravel's Enhanced Inventory[/b] and [b]Paravel's Enhanced Tooltips[/b]. Each one works without the others.
description=[/list]
